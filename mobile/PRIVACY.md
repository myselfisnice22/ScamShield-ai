## What ScamShield does
ScamShield AI checks messages and screenshots for signs of scams.

## Data we process
* **Account details (only if sign-in is turned on).** Sign-in is provided by
  [Clerk](https://clerk.com). Clerk stores your email address, name and sign-in details
  under Clerk's own privacy policy. ScamShield receives your name, email and a user ID
  to show who is signed in and to limit how many scans each account can send.
  ScamShield never sees your password. [Explain how to delete an account.]
* **Messages and screenshots you choose to scan.** They are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS)
  connection to the ScamShield server, checked, and the result is sent back.
  The server **does not store** them and does not log their contents.
* **Your IP address** is kept in the server's memory for about one minute to stop
  abuse (limiting how many scans can be sent per minute). It is not saved.
  [Also list anything your hosting provider logs.]
* **Scan history** is **off by default**. If you turn it on, a short preview (up to 80
  characters) of each scan is stored **only on your phone**. You can delete it at any time
  in the History screen. It is not included in phone backups.

## What we do NOT do
* We do not sell or share your data.
* We do not use advertising or tracking.
* We do not open links found in messages.
* We do not access your contacts.

## Your choices
Do not scan messages containing passwords, PINs, OTPs or other secrets. Crop screenshots to
remove private information before uploading.

## Important
ScamShield is experimental. A low-risk result never proves that a message is safe.

## Children
ScamShield does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes
We will update this page if the way ScamShield handles data changes.
